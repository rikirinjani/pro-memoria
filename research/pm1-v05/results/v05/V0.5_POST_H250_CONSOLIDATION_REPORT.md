# V0.5 Post-H250 Consolidation Report

Offline consolidation date: August 18, 2026.

## Executive Conclusion

The persisted H10-H250 dataset is internally complete at 26,100 calls, shows a clear token-scaling separation between the PM-1 packet condition (P) and the conversational condition (C), and preserves bounded PM-1 transmitted context while conversational context grows sharply with horizon. Across the completed dataset, PM-1 cumulative token growth is approximately linear over the measured horizons, while the conversational condition is strongly super-linear and is closely fit by a quadratic model. The evidence is sufficient to support the V0.5 state-handoff claim without executing H500/H1000.

## Threats To Validity

- This is a state-handoff benchmark, not a knowledge-accumulation benchmark.
- The results support bounded state transmission and token-economics separation in this benchmark only.
- The fitted trends are measured-horizon characterizations, not formal asymptotic proofs.
- The study does not compare PM-1 against compacted or summarized conversational baselines.
- The study is single-model and does not establish cross-model generalization.

## What V0.5 Establishes

- PM-1 transmission remains bounded in this benchmark.
- P cumulative token cost grows approximately linearly over the measured horizons.
- C grows strongly super-linearly over the measured horizons and is closely fit by a quadratic model.
- H250 shows roughly 90% token reduction relative to C in this benchmark.
- H250 achieved 100% state integrity in the persisted dataset.
- Model/output failures occurred and were captured by the harness.

## What V0.5 Does Not Establish

- Universal superiority over conversational memory.
- Asymptotically proven O(N) versus O(N^2).
- Superiority over compacted or summarized conversation.
- Cross-model generalization.
- Institutional-knowledge transmission.
- Semantic knowledge preservation beyond state continuity in this benchmark.
- A causal claim that PM-1 prevents all model failures.
- Real-world production economics.

## Recommended Next Experiment

A later phase should target knowledge transmission or institutional-memory transfer explicitly. That should be framed as a new experiment rather than a reinterpretation of V0.5.

## Suggested Results Wording

Across 26,100 completed handoffs spanning horizons 10, 25, 50, 100, and 250, the PM-1 packet condition maintained bounded transmitted context and exhibited approximately linear cumulative token growth. The conversational transcript condition exhibited strongly super-linear cumulative token growth and was closely fit by a quadratic model over the measured horizons. At horizon 250, the PM-1 condition consumed 3,672,305 cumulative tokens versus 38,366,105 for the conversational condition, a 90.43% reduction.

## Suggested Discussion Wording

These results should be interpreted as evidence about state-handoff transmission economics and state continuity, not as a general result about long-context reasoning or institutional knowledge preservation. Pro Memoria does not primarily compress known history; it skips retransmission of known state and transmits what the next worker needs to continue. Within this benchmark, that architectural difference produced bounded transmitted context and a large measured reduction in cumulative token cost relative to retransmitting the full conversational trajectory.
